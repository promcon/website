## Anirudha Jadhav
<br>

Anirudha Jadhav is an accomplished engineering leader and open-source advocate with over 17 years of experience in distributed systems, search technologies, and data analytics. Currently, he serves as an Sr. Engineering Leader at Amazon Web Services (AWS), where he leads the development of cloud-native, technology-agnostic platforms for insights and visualization within the OpenSearch project .
