## Victoria Nduka
<br>

Victoria Nduka is an evolving creative with diverse interests. In her relatively short career, she’s brewed beer as a supply chain management intern at Nigerian Breweries, designed digital experiences as a UX designer, and is now exploring cloud engineering—because why not?

Victoria dreams of traveling the world, and maybe even finishing that one crochet project she abandoned. Until then, she’s making an impact from her little corner of the world, through writing, speaking, and volunteering.
