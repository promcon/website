## Saswata Mukherjee
<br>

Saswata Mukherjee is a Senior Software Engineer at Red Hat, currently working on the OpenShift multi-cluster observability team. He is a maintainer of Thanos, Perses, and a Prometheus team member and loves contributing to several other open-source Go projects and libraries. Saswata’s interests include Go, Kubernetes, Prometheus, and open-source software. Avid book reader in free time.
