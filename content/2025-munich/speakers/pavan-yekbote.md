## Pavan Yekbote
<br>

Pavan Yekbote is a Software Engineer at AWS working on the OpenSearch Project. He primarily works on OpenSearch's machine learning plugins (ml-commons). He is passionate about distributed systems, machine learning, and big data. He works closely with at the cross-section of OpenSearch, OpenTelemetry and Agentic AI.
