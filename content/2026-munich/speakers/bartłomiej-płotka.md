## Bartłomiej Płotka
<br>

Bartek Płotka is a Senior Software Engineer Tech Lead at Google. SWE by heart, with an SRE background, currently working on Cloud Observability. Previously Principal Software Engineer at Red Hat. Author of "Efficient Go" book with O'Reilly. Serial CNCF mentor. As the co-founder of the CNCF Thanos project, Prometheus steering committee member and core maintainer of various open-source projects, he enjoys building OSS communities and maintainable, reliable distributed systems.
