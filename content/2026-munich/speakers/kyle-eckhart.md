## Kyle Eckhart
<br>

Kyle Eckhart is a seasoned software engineer with over a decade of industry experience. His work has centered on designing and building data ingestion platforms, scalable APIs, and system observability tools. He is currently works for Grafana Labs, focused on Grafana Alloy, Grafana’s OpenTelemetry distribution. 

Outside of work, Kyle is a proud father of two boys. He’s also an avid hiker, mosh pit enthusiast, and a board/video gamer.
