## Rushabh Shah
<br>

Rushabh Shah is a Docker Captain (one of ~220 globally) and Grafana Champion (inaugural cohort of ~110 worldwide) who helps engineering teams tame complexity in cloud-native infrastructure — from container orchestration to full-stack observability.

He works across the full cloud-native stack, from CI/CD and container security to distributed tracing and GitOps. In production, his work has driven a 59% reduction in AWS cloud spend, brought average release times down to ~10 minutes through end-to-end pipeline automation, and delivered a full cloud migration to Oracle Cloud Infrastructure with zero customer-facing downtime under a fixed business deadline.

Rushabh is an active open-source contributor and community builder. He organizes Grafana & Friends Ahmedabad, serves on the CNCG Gandhinagar core team, and has reached 1,000+ engineers through local cloud-native meetups. He represented KodeKloud at KubeCon India 2024, publishes technical content on Kubernetes, observability, and cloud cost optimization across LinkedIn, YouTube, and a bi-weekly newsletter, and mentors engineers worldwide — making complex infrastructure concepts accessible to everyone.
