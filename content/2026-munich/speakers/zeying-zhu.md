## Zeying Zhu
<br>

Zeying Zhu is a PhD candidate advised by Prof. Alan (Zaoxing) Liu at University of Maryland, College Park. Her research interests lie broadly in data systems, distributed systems, and approximate algorithms. Specifically, she is interested in designing low-latency, cost-efficient, highly scalable, and trustworthy approximate computing systems across the full cloud telemetry lifecycle — collection, transmission, storage, and analytics — using sketching, sampling, and sublinear algorithms together with system-stack co-design.
