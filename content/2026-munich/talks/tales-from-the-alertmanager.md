## Tales from the Alertmanager

After last year's Promcon it was clear Alertmanager needed some care. We have established a new set of maintainers, and have an agreed upon roadmap. In this talk we would like to:
- Introduce the maintainer team
- Mention what's new in Alertmanager this year
- Talk about the roadmap ahead
- Talk about how to engage and contribute to Alertmanager

Guido Trotter - Software Engineer at HRT
Siavash Safi - Software engineer at Cloudfare
Ethan Hunter - Software engineer at HRT
Solomon Jacobs - Alertmanager Maintainer
